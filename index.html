Good to know

How ordering, delivery, payment and fittings work at Mave Collection.

How ordering works

Ready-made dresses: choose a size, add the dress to your cart and check out with your name, phone number and delivery address.

Made to measure and custom designs: send us your measurements and, if you like, photos of the style you have in mind.

For every order we call or message you on WhatsApp to confirm the details, the price and the timing before anything is made or sent.

Delivery and pick-up

You can collect your dress from us in Danfa, Accra, or we can deliver it:

  • within Accra and Tema,
  • by courier to other parts of Ghana, and
  • internationally, quoted for each order.

The delivery fee depends on where you are. We tell you the amount when we confirm your order.

Payment

Ready-made dresses are paid for in full.

Made-to-measure and custom dresses start with a deposit, and the balance is paid before your dress is delivered or collected.

We confirm the amounts and how to pay when we contact you, so you never pay anything before we have spoken.

How long it takes

Ready-made dresses are prepared once your order is confirmed. Made-to-measure timing depends on the dress and how much detail it has, so we confirm the time with you when we call, before you pay a deposit.

Fittings, exchanges and returns

Made-to-measure and custom dresses are made for one person, so they cannot be returned. If the fit is not right, we adjust it for you free of charge.

Ready-made dresses can be exchanged if they have not been worn.

Message us on WhatsApp as soon as you have tried your dress and we will arrange it.

Your measurements and photos

We use your measurements and style photos only to make your dress. They are kept in a private database that only our team can open. You can ask us to delete them at any time. Read our Privacy Notice.
